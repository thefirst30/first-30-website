Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

Who we are

The First 30™ runs mental performance and breathwork training sessions in Sydney, NSW, Australia. You can contact us about privacy at hello@thefirst30.com.au.

What we collect and how

  • Contact and booking details. When you book, join a waitlist, submit a form or contact us, we may collect your name, email address, mobile number, booking and attendance history, and emergency contact details if you provide them. Please check that your emergency contact is comfortable with you sharing their details.
  • Payments. Stripe processes card payments. We may receive payment status, receipts and transaction details to manage purchases, memberships or refunds. We do not store full card numbers on our servers.
  • Health information. With your consent, we may collect health information you choose to share with a facilitator where it is reasonably necessary for safe participation. This can include a condition or limitation relevant to the session.
  • Website and booking technology. Our website host and embedded services may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details. Embedded services may use cookies or similar technology for functions such as booking and security.

We may need certain details to confirm a booking, process a payment or contact you about a session. If we lack information needed to assess whether an activity is safe for you, we may ask you to modify or skip that activity.

How we use and share information

  • To manage bookings, attendance, waitlists, passes, memberships, payments and refunds, and to respond to enquiries.
  • To send service messages such as booking confirmations, arrival instructions and reminders by email or SMS.
  • To support safe participation. We limit access to health information to people who need it for that purpose. Where necessary and permitted by law, we may share relevant details with emergency services or use the emergency contact you provide.
  • To send occasional updates or offers where we have your consent. You can unsubscribe using the instructions in the message or email us. We do not use health information for marketing.

Our service providers include Bookamat for bookings, Stripe for payments, Jotform for online forms, Google Workspace for business communications and GitHub Pages for website hosting. We may also disclose information where required or authorised by law.

Overseas processing

Some providers may process or store personal information outside Australia, including in the United States. Locations can vary by provider and service. We take reasonable steps to protect information disclosed overseas in line with applicable Australian privacy requirements.

Security, retention and your choices

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from loss, misuse and unauthorised access, changes or disclosure. We retain it for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes above and to meet applicable legal obligations. When we no longer need it and are not required to retain it, we take reasonable steps to destroy or de-identify it.

Email us to request access, correction or deletion. We may need to verify your identity. We will consider deletion requests in light of any legal obligations and other lawful reasons to retain the information.

Privacy questions and complaints

Email hello@thefirst30.com.au with your concern and how you would like it resolved. We will review it and aim to respond within 30 days. If you are dissatisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner for matters within its jurisdiction. For NSW health information matters, you can contact the Information and Privacy Commission NSW.
