A few things to know

What exactly will I do?

A seated, coach-led mental fitness session combining paced breathing, sound-based attention exercises, working memory and cognitive flexibility practice, and guided recovery. You can skip any exercise.

Is this meditation or therapy?

No. This is research-informed mental fitness practice, not clinical treatment or a validated assessment. The combined programme has not yet been independently validated. Explore the research.

What happens after the preview?

Regular Wednesday sessions are planned from 6 January 2027. Single session $35; three sessions $79; monthly $99. There is no obligation to join.
